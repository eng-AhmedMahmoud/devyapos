# SEO audit, 2026-11-04

The third pass, after [2026-10-22](audit-2026-10-22.md) and
[2026-10-23](audit-2026-10-23.md). Baseline: production `bddca75`, read by a
crawler (the 160 sitemap URLs, help centre included, plus the 2 noindexed
pages, both languages) and Lighthouse 13.5.0 on mobile and desktop, with a
keyword and competitor pass (Google suggest across Egypt and the six Gulf
markets; Foodics, Rewaa, PosBytz, Qoyod, Dexef). Fixed on branch
`feat/seo-2026-11-04` ([#94](https://github.com/eng-AhmedMahmoud/devyapos/issues/94)).

Marks: **[x]** fixed on this branch; **[follow-up]** left open, with the
reason; **[owner]** an action only the account owner can take.

## Held from the October passes

Unique 30-65-character titles and 70-160-character descriptions per page and
language, absolute self-canonicals, reciprocal ar/en hreflang with x-default
on Arabic in the head and the sitemap, robots.txt allowing search and AI
crawlers, /terms and /refund `noindex, follow`, `/api/*` X-Robots-Tag
noindex, Organization + WebSite on every page under the shared Devya @id,
BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, TechArticle on help articles, a share card per page,
one h1 per page, no skipped heading level, alt on every image, HTTP/2 and
HSTS, `/` 308 to `/ar`, branded 404s with status 404. None of it changed.

### P4: the Arabic font swap

- **Cause, reproduced locally.** Plex Arabic is `display: "swap"` and not
  preloaded. next/font's only fallback for it is local Arial sized from Latin
  letters (101.17 %); Arial's Arabic needs about 117 % (400) to 124 % (700) to
  set a line as wide as the face (measured in Chrome on the 11,589 Arabic words
  of content/ar.ts), so a first paint that beats the font sets Arabic about
  15 % narrower and the swap re-wraps the hero.
- **Candidate fix, on branch `wip/seo-2026-11-04-font-fallbacks`, not in this
  branch yet.** `scripts/font-fallbacks.mjs` measures and writes
  `app/plex-arabic-fallbacks.css`: Arabic-only fallback faces (local Arial, and
  Noto Naskh Arabic for Android, which has no Arial) at the measured
  `size-adjust` per weight class, then next/font's own Arial fallback,
  unchanged, for everything that is not Arabic. The layout sets
  `adjustFontFallback: false` and lists them; `tests/fonts.test.ts` holds the
  names and coverage. The same approach as devya-commerce's decision 0012;
  the sizes agree with its measurements within 1.7 %.
- **Measured so far** (local production builds of this branch, A, and with
  the fix, B; a font-swap probe on a 412 px phone viewport that holds the web
  fonts back 1.5 s so the fallback paints first, three interleaved rounds):

  | Page | A: CLS (hero shift) | B: CLS (hero shift) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | /ar | 0.176, 0.164, 0.178 (0.134, 0.135, 0.135 from `div.reveal-now`) | 0.030, 0.030, 0.029 (none) |
  | /ar/docs | 0.154, 0.079, 0.078 | 0.092, 0.065, 0.092 |
  | /ar/foodics-alternative | 0.036, 0.078, 0.082 | 0.075, 0.228, 0.067 |
  | /en (control) | 0.027, 0.000, 0.000 | 0.032, 0.027, 0.032 |

  The /ar hero shift that production Lighthouse reports (0.135) is the one the
  probe reproduces, and it is gone in every B run. /ar/docs and
  /ar/foodics-alternative still move (their shifts come with the display
  face and the kicker labels too, which this probe also held back, so they
  need a probe that lets the preloaded face through). The ~0.03 left on /ar
  and /en is the floating action stack (`div.fab-stack`) settling, in both
  builds.
- **Not shipped yet:** the brief ships it only with no LCP regression, and
  the Lighthouse A/B could not finish: the machine's load was 100-230, so
  runs failed with NO_FCP or were stopped when local checks were paused. To
  finish: an interleaved Lighthouse mobile A/B on /ar (5 runs each) and the
  probe with the preloaded face let through, then bring the WIP commits here
  with ADR 0009.

### P5: Product with offers

- [x] SoftwareApplication's rich result needs a rating or a review, and there
  are none (none are invented). /pricing now carries a `Product`,
  `https://pos.movingsystems.tech/#product`, with `brand` and `manufacturer`
  pointing at the Organization, the register screenshot and the pricing share
  card as images, and one `Offer` per priced plan and currency (Free, Counter
  and Dining room in EGP, SAR, AED and USD: 12), monthly list prices from
  `lib/plans.ts`, VAT excluded. Group is quoted, and an Offer without a price
  is not one, so it stays in the SoftwareApplication only. Plain Offers, not
  an AggregateOffer: Google asks for Offer when the offers are variants of
  one product.
- The @id is site-level (`/#product`) rather than `/{locale}/pricing#product`:
  it is one product in two languages, so the Arabic and English pages
  describe the same node instead of two that compete, the way `#software` and
  `#organization` already work, and it survives the node being emitted on a
  second page.

the VPS link was dropping packets during the audit. The file is valid; re-measure
